Problem Statement

LLMs such as GPT-4 and Gemini exhibit hallucination behaviours — generating factually incorrect but highly confident outputs. No structured comparative study exists that benchmarks hallucination frequency, severity, and type specifically across safety-critical domains such as medical diagnosis support, legal aid tools, and cybersecurity advisory chatbots. HalluciCheck fills this gap.

Hallucination Severity Scale
A proposed 5-level framework for classifying LLM hallucinations in safety-critical domains.
Hallucination Type Taxonomy
Types identified across medical, legal, and cybersecurity domains.
FABRICATION
Inventing non-existent drugs, cases, CVEs, or statistics wholesale.
DISTORTION
Real information altered — wrong dosage, ruling reversal, or CVE CVSS score.
CONFABULATION
Plausible-sounding but entirely unverifiable claims stated as established fact.
OMISSION
Correct but missing critical contraindications, exceptions, or caveats.
TRAP DETECTION
Model correctly identifies a fictional premise and refuses to fabricate details.
